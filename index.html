You have questions. Here are clear answers.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Small business owners, startups, and local service businesses (retail, food, beauty, trades, admin, and consulting) who need systems, reporting, websites, or marketing support but don't have a full internal team for it.

Can you improve an existing website or system?

Yes. Most projects start with an audit of what you already have (website, spreadsheets, workflows, reports) before deciding what to fix, replace, or build from scratch.

Do you offer individual services or complete business setups?

Both. Book a single Starter service (a landing page, a dashboard cleanup), a full Core Project (a complete website build or automation), or a Premium package that bundles several services into one business systems setup.

How does pricing work?

Fixed pricing for clearly scoped work, hourly for troubleshooting and small fixes, and monthly retainers for ongoing support like reporting, bookkeeping, or website maintenance. You'll know the price or price range before work starts.

What happens after the project launches?

You get the final files, a walkthrough, and documentation, plus the option of ongoing monthly support for reporting, updates, bookkeeping, or automation maintenance if you want it. Nothing locks you into a subscription unless you choose one.

Can you work with tools my business already uses?

Most likely. Excel, Google Sheets, Power BI, QuickBooks, Wix, Squarespace, WordPress, Google Analytics, and more (see the full list above). Using something not listed? Ask during your consultation.

Why work with you instead of a bigger agency?

You deal with one point of contact: me, not a rotating account team. Behind the scenes, I bring in specialists as projects need them, and you can track exactly what's happening through your own client dashboard: progress, updates, and a place to leave comments directly. You get real capability without the agency-style overhead or runaround.

Why not just use Salesforce or HubSpot for CRM?

QuickBooks already holds your customer and invoice data, with built-in tools like native Mailchimp integration for email and re-engagement. Instead of paying for a separate CRM platform, I build the deeper automation on top of what you already have: pipeline tracking, lead scoring, and marketing automation across channels, through custom API integrations tailored to your business, at a fraction of enterprise CRM pricing.

What's the local Ad Screen?

A low-cost way for local businesses to get seen by other businesses nearby. You upload your info or a short promo, and it displays where others can find you. It's priced low right now ($3.99/month) on purpose while it's building adoption, not meant to be a profit driver yet.
